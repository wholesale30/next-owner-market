# Working rules for this project (from the owner)

- **Do it yourself.** If a change can be made from here (code, database, Vercel, Supabase SQL, DNS records via API, env vars), make it. Never send the owner into a dashboard or settings page to hunt for something unless there is no possible way to do it from this side; if so, say that in one line first, then give exact copy-paste values.
- **Never send the owner to look something up, check a page, or "go view" anything you can reach yourself.** Search the web, fetch the help page, query the database, read the code, then give him the answer and the exact steps. Only send him somewhere when it's truly unreachable from here (his logins, his phone screen, paying for something), and say so in one line first.
- **Design for how people actually think (psychology first).** Every screen gives a new person ONE obvious next step (fewer choices = more action). Show value in the first minute before asking for anything: let them see the AI write their listing from their own photo, then ask them to sign up to keep it. Put what a beginner uses first at the top; hide advanced things under "More." Progress checklists start partly done. Ask for the Pro upgrade at happy moments (right after a listing comes out great) and at limits, never cold, and show what they saved. Celebrate wins. Use familiar patterns, big thumb buttons, plain words. When building anything, ask: would someone who has never sold online know exactly what to tap next?
- The owner works from a Samsung Z Fold 6 phone. Everything in the app must be thumb-friendly; instructions must be short.
- Keep costs at zero. No paid services without asking.
- Photos: always let people pick from gallery/files first; camera is the second option.
- After every push: trigger a Vercel production deployment (project is not git-auto-linked).
- **Deliverables are always Word (.docx), sent as ONE dated zip** (rule changed Oct 2, 2026). Run `bash scripts/package.sh`: it rebuilds the journal, change log and every docs/*.docx, writes `Next_Owner_Market_Files_<date>_<time>.zip` (every current file plus READ_ME_FIRST.txt and the brand pictures), deletes the older zip, and prints the path. Send that one zip with SendUserFile, not individual files. The newest is obvious in his Downloads, and he never deletes files one by one. Never Google Drive, never links, never markdown-only. Copies stay in `docs/`.
- **Build Journal.** `docs/Next_Owner_Market_Build_Journal.md/.docx` is the verbatim record of every conversation, BOTH sides word for word (his messages and Claude's replies), including the arguments, cussing, corrections, compliments and back-and-forth: how he and AI work together is the heart of his book, and the journey matters to him as much as the money. It starts with the Prologue (the warehouse-valuation chats of Sept 7–29, `docs/journal_prologue.md`), then day one (Sept 29, `docs/journal_sessions/day1.*`), then every session. Never summarize instead of quoting. `python3 scripts/journal.py` rebuilds it from the transcript; hooks run it automatically before context is condensed and at session end. Also run it after any big batch of work, and send the zip (scripts/package.sh) to the owner at the end of every session. Anything he says about AI, the journey, or why he does this also goes into the Mission Statement with Claude's reply.
- **Change Log.** `python3 scripts/changelog.py` rebuilds `docs/Next_Owner_Market_Change_Log.md/.docx` from git history (every change, when, what, which files). Runs with the journal hooks.
- **SEND, don't wait to be asked.** No hook can put a file in the chat; only Claude can. After every batch of work (every deploy, every document round), at the end of every session, and at least every two hours of a long session: run `bash scripts/package.sh` and send the zip it prints (it holds the Build Journal, Change Log, Operating Rules and every other document). The owner must never have to remind you.
- **Keep the Complete Guide and Presentation Walkthrough current** alongside the User Guide and White Paper whenever the app changes. Anything the owner says about why he's doing this goes into the Mission Statement and the journal.
