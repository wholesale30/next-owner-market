# The bottom line

The paid thrift apps make money by **charging people to scan**, from \$9.99 a week to \$29.99 a month. Their reviews are full of three complaints:

- wrong prices;
- slow scans;
- trial tricks, like getting charged during a "free trial" or after cancelling.

**We make money a different way: when people sell.** That lets us give the scan away. Each free answer brings in a shopper, and each "I bought it" becomes a listing we earn on.

# What "Paid \$4, sells for \$45, \$31 profit" means

You're at Goodwill holding a lamp with a **\$4** tag. Buy or Pass says it sells for about **\$45** used. After the selling site's fees and shipping, you'd keep about **\$31**. That's a BUY.

The **share card** is a small picture of that result that people post to show off a find. The post carries our web address and their invite link, so every brag is free advertising for us.

# How we make money

1. **Selling fees.** Every "I bought it" becomes a listing in our store, where we take our cut when it sells. Listing is free. This is how Mercari and Whatnot make money: on sales, not scans.
2. **Pro.** Unlimited checks, unlimited AI listings for nine sites, and the selling tools. We ask at happy moments only: right after a great BUY, or when today's free checks run out.
3. **What a scan costs us** is pennies of AI, so giving away 5 a day is cheap. One paying Pro member covers hundreds of free checks.

# Next builds (in order)

1. **"Was this right?"** thumbs up or down on every answer, plus asking what it actually sold for. This improves accuracy, our biggest edge.
2. **Monthly "Flip Wrapped":** "You found \$412 this month. Best find: a \$3 Pyrex bowl." People remember the best moment and how things ended.
3. **Weekly thrift streak** with one free skip. This is what made Duolingo sticky.
4. **Barcode scan** for books, games and electronics.
5. **Bins mode:** check five items in a row without leaving the camera.
6. **Invite reward for both sides:** you and your friend each get 20 extra free checks. Dropbox grew 3,900% this way.
