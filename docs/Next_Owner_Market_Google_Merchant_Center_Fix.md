# Bottom line

Google emailed at 2:14 AM on October 5, 2026: the Merchant Center account (ID 5863082353) was flagged for **Misrepresentation**, so free listings stopped showing on Google Shopping. The rest of the site and Google search are not affected.

**Everything on the website side is fixed and live.** Four steps are left, and they're behind your Google login, so only you can do them. Then wait 2–3 days and ask Google for a review **once**. Each failed review makes the next wait longer, so don't rush it.

# What was wrong, and what was fixed

| Problem Google looks for | What we had | Fixed |
|---|---|---|
| Who runs the business | Terms said "operated by ____________" and "Contact: ____________" (blank) | Filled in: Next Owner Market, Richmond, VA, phone and email |
| Contact page | None | New **/contact**: big Call, Text and Email buttons |
| Return and refund policy | Only inside the Terms | New **/returns** page in plain words |
| Shipping policy | None | New **/shipping**: where, cost, handling 1–3 business days, delivery 2–8 days, pickup |
| About the business | None | New **/about** |
| Policy links on every page | Only on the home page | Footer on every public page: Contact · About · Returns · Shipping · Terms · Privacy |
| Only your own items in a standard account | The feed sent items from Nikki and Gwynn too. Google's rule: other sellers' items need a special "Marketplace" account | The feed now sends **only items Next Owner Market sells itself** |
| Honest delivery | Pickup-only items were sent as "$0 shipping," which Google reads as free delivery | Pickup-only items are left out of the Google feed (they still show on our site) |
| Honest condition | "Like new" was sent as "refurbished" (to Google, that means professionally restored) | Sent as "used." "For parts" items are left out |
| Shipping cost shown | None for most items | The most it could cost anywhere in the US, so a buyer never pays more than Google showed |
| Unfinished look | Stray `**` marks in Terms | Cleaned up |

Item pages now also say, for your own items: "Sold and shipped by Next Owner Market, Richmond, VA. Not as described? Full refund." with links to Returns, Shipping and Contact.

# Policies I wrote. Check these two

1. **Returns:** not as described, damaged or never arrived = full refund including shipping, reported within 3 days after delivery (the same 3 days already in the Terms). If we need it back, **we send a prepaid return label**. No returns for change of mind. *If you want to offer more (say 14-day returns on your own items), tell me and I'll change the page.*
2. **Shipping:** items you sell **ship within 1–3 business days** after payment. *If the warehouse needs longer, tell me the number.*

# Your 4 steps in Merchant Center (merchants.google.com)

These are behind your Google login; I can't reach them from here.

## 1. Business details

**Settings (gear) → Business info → Edit business details**

- Business name: **Next Owner Market**
- Business address: the real address you'd put on a check (Richmond, VA). It must be real; Google may mail a code or check your ID.
- Customer service:
  - Website: **https://nextownermarket.com/contact**
  - Email: **shayne@shayneforva.com**
  - Phone: **804-720-7910**
- Save after each section.

## 2. Return policy

**Products & store → Shipping and returns → Return policies → Add return policy**

- Return policy URL: **https://nextownermarket.com/returns**
- Countries: **United States**
- Accept returns: **Defective products only**
- Accept exchanges: **No**
- Product condition: **New and slightly used products**
- Return window: **3** days
- Return method: **By mail**, with a **free** return label
- Restocking fee: **No cost**
- Refund processing time: **10** days

## 3. Identity check

If it asks you to verify your identity, upload your driver's license. The name must match the account.

## 4. Wait, then request one review

Wait **2–3 days** (until **Thursday, October 8**) so Google re-reads the new pages. Then go to **Needs attention** (or the banner on the Home page) → the Misrepresentation issue → **Request review**. In the box, paste:

> We added a Contact page, About page, Return and refund policy, and Shipping policy, linked from every page. The Terms now name the operator and contact details. Our product feed now lists only items we sell and ship ourselves, with accurate shipping costs, handling times and item condition.

A review takes 3–7 business days.

# Later: listing other sellers' items on Google

To put Nikki's and other sellers' items on Google Shopping, Google requires a **Marketplace multi-client account** (one sub-account per seller). That's a bigger setup. It's worth doing once there are many sellers, not now.
