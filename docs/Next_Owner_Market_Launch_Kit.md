# Next Owner Market — 30-Day Launch Kit

*September 29, 2026. Goal for the first 30 days: 500 free sign-ups, 50 Pro subscribers, 100 store orders in Virginia.*

---

## 1. The pitch in one line

**Photograph the pile. Get the listings.** Upload 40 photos, the AI sorts them into items, cleans the backgrounds, writes every listing, and gives you copy-paste versions for nine marketplaces, plus your own store where buyers pay by card. $15/month. Three free to try.

Landing page: **nextownermarket.com/pro**

## 2. The demo video (60–90 seconds, shot on your phone, no editing needed)

Record your screen with the phone's built-in screen recorder, or point a second phone at the first. Talk while you do it. Don't script it word for word; hit these beats.

1. **(0–10s) The pile.** Camera on a table with 8–10 random things: a drill, a lamp, a receiver, a few kitchen gadgets. "Every reseller has this pile. Watch."
2. **(10–25s) Dump a batch.** Open Snap mode, tap Upload photos from gallery, pick 20 photos. "I took these in about a minute walking around the table."
3. **(25–40s) It sorts.** Show the groups appear. "It figured out which photos are the same item. That's the drill, that's the lamp." Tap Finish.
4. **(40–60s) The listings.** Open one item. Show the clean background, the title, description, specs, price range. "I didn't type any of that."
5. **(60–75s) Nine marketplaces.** Scroll the copy blocks. Tap Copy on Facebook. Tap the How-to-post button. "Facebook, eBay, OfferUp, Craigslist, Mercari, Poshmark, Vinted, Depop, Etsy. And it tells you how to post if you've never done it."
6. **(75–90s) The store.** Show the item live at nextownermarket.com with the Buy now button. "Or they just buy it here, by card, and the money's held till pickup. Three free listings to try it. Link's below."

Post it: your Facebook group, the reseller groups (section 4), TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, X. Same video everywhere. Caption is the one-line pitch plus the link.

## 4. Creator outreach (5–10 small reseller YouTubers / TikTokers)

Target channels with 5k–50k subscribers who post "what sold this week" or "thrift haul" videos. They're cheap, their audience is exactly right, and they answer DMs. Offer $100–300 for a walkthrough, or free Pro for a year plus a referral link (they earn a month for every signup).

**Message:**

>

## 5. Product Hunt (one day, free, brings a few hundred tech-savvy sellers)

- Make an account 2 weeks before launch; be active (comment, upvote).
- Launch on a Tuesday–Thursday, 12:01 AM Pacific.
- Tagline: "Photograph the pile. Get the listings."
- First comment from you: the story (30 years, warehouse, built it in a weekend with AI, opened it up).
- Ask your list and group to upvote that morning.

## 6. Your own channels

- **Facebook group:** pin the demo video. Weekly "new arrivals" post that links to the store.
- **Email list:** New Arrivals blast every Thursday (📧 Email in the app). Thursday evening is when Marketplace traffic peaks for the weekend.
- **Store link** in every Facebook/Craigslist/OfferUp listing you paste (the copy blocks already include it).
- **QR tag** on every item at the warehouse: walk-ins scan and buy on the spot.
- **Referral link:** every seller has one on their home screen. Push it in the group: "invite a friend, both get a month free."

---

## 7. Local buyers (Virginia first)

- Post 5 items a day to Facebook Marketplace with the store link. Marketplace is your top-of-funnel; the store is where they land.
- Craigslist: renew every 48 hours.
- Nextdoor: one post a week, "warehouse open Saturday, here's what's new," with the store link.
- Saturday warehouse hours, posted in the group and on Nextdoor. Every walk-in becomes a subscriber (sign-up box on the store, or you type them in).
- Local Facebook: Richmond, Charlottesville, Lynchburg, Roanoke buy/sell/trade groups. Post items, not ads.

## 8. What to measure (check weekly)

| Number | Where | Target, day 30 |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-ups | People page | 500 |
| Pro subscribers | Stripe dashboard → Subscriptions | 50 |
| Store orders | Orders page | 100 |
| Subscribers on the email list | Subscribers page | 1,000 |
| Store sales $ | Money page | $5,000 |

If sign-ups are high and Pro is low: the free tier is too generous or the upgrade prompt is buried. If sign-ups are low: the video isn't reaching people; post more places, pay more creators.

## 9. The 30-day calendar

**Week 1 (setup):** shoot the demo video · join the groups · set Settings (city, contact, address for the email footer) · list 30 of your own items · $1 test order · ask 3 friends to sign up and list something.
**Week 2 (launch):** post the video in 5 groups a day · DM 10 creators · Product Hunt · first New Arrivals blast · Facebook group pinned post.
**Week 3 (push):** creator videos go live · second blast · post in the remaining groups · reply to every comment and message within the hour.
**Week 4 (fix and repeat):** fix whatever people complained about most · third blast · post "what sold this month" in the group · start week 2 again with the next set of groups.

Creators $500–2,000 (optional) · everything else $0. Stripe and Resend are pay-as-you-go and covered by the first few Pro subscriptions.
