# The bottom line

- **Most Pro members make us money.** A typical Pro member costs about **\$4.30 a month** in AI. After card fees we keep about **\$9.80 of the \$15 (66%)**.
- **"Unlimited" is the leak.** A heavy user (a warehouse reseller listing 300 items a month) costs about **\$26 in AI**, so we **lose about \$12 a month** on them. Nothing stops one person from costing \$50 or more.
- **Thrift Pro (\$3.99) can lose money too.** Someone scanning 20 items every day costs about \$9 a month.
- **The fix:** keep the price, give Pro a big monthly allowance, and sell top-ups to the few who need more. Then cut the AI bill 30–40% with cheaper settings nobody will notice. Details are below.

# What I recommend

## 1. Pro stays \$15. "Unlimited" becomes a big monthly allowance.

- **Pro includes 300 AI uses a month** (listings, lookups, piles and Buy or Pass all count as one each). Our worst cost is about \$9, so Pro never loses money. A typical member uses about 150 and never sees the limit.
- **The first 2 fixes on every lookup are free.** After that, a fix counts as a use. That stops runaway re-checks without punishing honest corrections.
- **Show it as progress, not a wall:** "214 of 300 used this month." At 80% they get a friendly heads-up with the top-up button.

## 2. Top-ups people can buy when they want them

| Pack | Price | Our cost (worst) | Card fee | We keep |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 more AI uses | \$6.99 | \$3.00 | 50¢ | **\$3.49 (50%)** |

| **"Power Seller" plan** instead of Pro: 1,000 a month | \$39/mo | \$30 worst, about \$15 typical | \$1.70 | **\$7.30–\$22.30** |
| Deep clean photos, 20 | \$2.99 | 68¢ | 39¢ | **\$1.92 (64%)** |
| Deep clean photos, 100 | \$9.99 | \$3.40 | 59¢ | **\$6.00 (60%)** |
| Thrift Pro (\$3.99) | keep the price | fair use: 30 checks a day | | most people use 5–10 |

Free for **you, staff, and anyone you comp**. The "comped" switch already exists on accounts.

## 3. Cut the AI bill 30–40% (nobody will notice)

| Change | Saves |
|---|---|
| **Help questions:** have the AI "remember" the User Guide between questions (prompt caching) | about 85% of the cost of each help question |
| **Buy or Pass and weight guesses on the cheaper Haiku model** (half price). It's fine for quick verdicts; listings and appraisals stay on the better model. | about 40% of each Buy or Pass |
| **Shrink photos for the AI to about 1,100 pixels.** Buyers still see the full-size photo; only the copy the AI reads is smaller. | about 30% of each photo's cost |
| **Snap mode runs overnight in a batch** (Batch pricing is half off). Listings are ready in the morning. | 50% on bulk listing |

## 4. See the spending (Operations page)

- Every AI call writes what it cost into a log.
- Operations shows **"AI spend this month: \$X,"** split by feature and by member, so you can tap any number and see who.
- You get an alert if any one member passes \$10 in a month.

That's how we know for sure instead of estimating.
